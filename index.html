Welcome to the Pitch Event Partner Dashboard! This tool is designed to help you monitor team engagement by tracking application progress and identifying teams that have started, but not yet submitted, their Diamond Challenge application so you can follow up and provide support.

The dashboard is updated weekly as teams continue working on their submissions, so you'll see the most current participation data available.

To view your teams, enter your pitch event location in the search bar (for example, Greece, Athens). Select your location from the dropdown list of matching results, and the dashboard will display all teams that selected your pitch event site during the application process.
